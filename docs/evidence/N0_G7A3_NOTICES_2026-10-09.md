# N0-G7a3: the third-party notices carry what Ninerr owes

Issue: #190 (N0 umbrella). This follows N0-G7a1 (the A/B source-rights audit) and N0-G7a2 (the Apache-2.0 declaration).

## Removed from `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
The founder's authorization lets donor-facing acknowledgements go where it covers them (`docs/provenance/FOUNDER_AUTHORIZATION_2026-10-08.md`). These are category A projects from which nothing was copied:
- **The guidance-only donors:** `mrmps/classifier-dev`, `kunchenguid/no-mistakes`, `Appllama/appllama-skills` and `reinaldosimoes/design-resources`.
- **The reference-only donors:** `kgoedecke/doop`, `firecrawl/firecrawl` and `caio0452/jev_search`.
- **The Paper.design section.** No Paper material is in the tree, and Paper's names are not used as Ninerr's identity.

These projects stay recorded in `docs/provenance/LICENSE_REGISTER.json` and in the packages' provenance. Their register entries no longer point at the notices.

## Kept
- **Every category B entry,** with its license and obligations:
  - the shipped dependencies (Parse5, Impeccable with its reproduced upstream NOTICE, Playwright for tests);
  - the Electron runtime;
  - the optional runtimes Ninerr does not ship;
  - the development tooling;
  - the independently licensed projects that were studied but not incorporated (`vcashwin/paper-snapshot` and the public `paper-design/*` repositories). Nothing may be copied from them unless its license is established and met.
- **The license notices of the category A code donors:** `unreallabsai/unreal-agent`, `bytedance/UI-TARS-desktop`, `kunchenguid/firstmate` (with `packages/agent-supervisor/NOTICE.md`) and `AhmadIbrahiim/Website-downloader`. Concepts from these projects were ported into Ninerr's source. The 2026-10-03 attestation keeps upstream license and NOTICE preservation, and the founder's later permission to remove donor material applies only "where covered by those authorizations". So these notices stay unless the founder supersedes that rule.

The file now speaks of Ninerr and states its Apache-2.0 license.

## Enforcement
`tests/license-register.test.mjs` checks the notices in both directions:
- every category B entry and every category A code donor is named;
- no other category A project is named, apart from `pbakaus/impeccable`, which appears as the upstream of the Impeccable dependency whose notice the file reproduces;
- names must match exactly, so `paper-design/paper` does not match `paper-design/paper-mono`;
- every register entry that points at the notices is named there.

## Also
- `tests/sbom.test.mjs` now covers the first-party fallback: a workspace package that declares no license is recorded as `NOASSERTION`, never guessed.
- `docs/CURRENT.md` names N0-G7a2 as the declaring grain.

Both were raised by the N0-G7a2 review.

`tests/license-register.test.mjs` also pins, by SHA-256, the two upstream texts the notices reproduce verbatim: Impeccable's `NOTICE.md` and Unreal Agent's MIT license. That was raised by the N0-G7a3 review.
